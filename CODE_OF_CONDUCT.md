# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Swahilipot Hub Foundation Code of Conduct**:

👉 <https://github.com/Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>

In short: be kind, assume good faith, be patient with beginners, and never make someone
feel small for not knowing something yet.

**Reporting a concern:** email **conduct@swahilipothub.co.ke**, or speak to any Swahilipot
Hub staff member in person. Reports are handled confidentially and without retaliation.
